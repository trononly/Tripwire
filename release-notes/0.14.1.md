## What's new
- Settings now shows whether M-Pesa automation is actually connected, not just switched on, with a retry action and recovery steps
- Copy a diagnostic report from Settings when automation needs troubleshooting
- Battery and background restrictions appear only when they can affect automation, with HyperOS Autostart and battery steps on Xiaomi devices
- Automation now stops safely after a disconnect or timeout and asks you to verify the M-Pesa transaction before trying again
- A second tap can no longer start a duplicate payment while the PIN or confirmation dialog is open
