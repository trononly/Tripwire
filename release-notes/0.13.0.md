## What's new
- A redesigned dashboard: one finding about your money, a four-cell balance strip, and a cashflow chart that follows the range you pick
- Spending is now one ranked, one-colour breakdown called "Where it went", with Standard and Custom views
- Light mode, in a complete warm-stone theme; money in and money out are distinct in both themes
- Every screen names itself and keeps a single primary action, and tablets keep the bottom bar instead of a squeezed sidebar
- Guided payments fill the "*334#" menu again: the dialog is an overlay owned by the phone app, and the automation now reads that window instead of its own screen
- Guided payments stop at the final "1 = Yes" prompt by default; Settings → Preferences → Auto-confirm after PIN answers it for you
- Dual-SIM payments go through the wallet's own phone account, with no "Call with SIM 1 / SIM 2" chooser in the way
- If Android stops running the automation while leaving it switched on, Send says so, and Preferences offers "Accessibility broken? Re-enable here"
- A two-SIM phone no longer ends up with four wallets: granting phone-state access mid-import used to file the same inbox twice
- Money out is red in the transaction list, matching the dashboard
- The app no longer flashes white or opens onto an empty dashboard; the splash stays up until the first read lands
- The dashboard keeps its rows on screen while it re-analyses, instead of tearing them down and replaying their animation
- Privacy Mode is repeatable: tap a veiled figure to reveal it, tap again to put the veil back
- Recurring payments found in your statements stay visible as you move between months
- The category editor puts the icon beside the name, with the icon pack in a focused dialog
- Settings opens with appearance, puts support last, and links Categories on mobile
- The balance chart has a teal trend, a period change and counterparty monograms
