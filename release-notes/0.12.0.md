## What's new
- Label transactions with your own emoji categories, and rename or re-icon them later
- See your own categories in the spending donut and filter the transaction list by them
- Pay Pochi, Paybill and Buy Goods on the SIM of the wallet selected in the header, with no "Call with SIM 1 / SIM 2" prompt
- Set up recurring payments yourself, with a fixed amount or "ask each time"
- Pay a recurring payment from its card: a fixed amount runs in one tap, a variable one asks for the amount
- Swipe between recurring payments with dots, edit one in place, or skip a cycle you paid in cash
- Get a notification on the due date, plus any number of minutes, hours, days or weeks before it
- Track a bill the app detected, or split a wide detected range into the amounts you actually pay
- Reset the app from Settings when you want a fresh slate
- Payment forms clear after a receipt lands, and a cancelled payment no longer leaves Pay now stuck
