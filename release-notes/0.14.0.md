## What's new
- Backup and restore: save a copy of your database to Google Drive, Downloads, or any folder, and restore a .db copy after a confirmation
- Set a monthly spending cap on any category and see how much is left
- Choose the percentage where a category starts warning; 80% is only the default, not a fixed rule
- A category turns amber at its warning point and red once spending passes the cap, and the dashboard warns when a budget is close to or over its cap
- Budgets follow the newest month in the active dataset, so a historical statement reports the month it actually covers
- The duplicate Categories shortcut in Settings is gone; use the sidebar or the dashboard donut
- Reset now leaves the app on the Import SMS screen instead of re-importing the whole inbox, and automatic history import stays paused until you tap Import SMS
- The arrival slip only appears for a payment started in Tripwire, shows the payment rather than its charge, and says "Payment sent" for money out; long-press still opens any transaction
